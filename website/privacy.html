TapTap Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

TapTap ("TapTap", "the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") is developed and operated by Batman Tech.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TapTap collects or processes, why that information is used, how it is stored and protected, when it may be shared, how long it may be retained, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect and Process

TapTap collects or processes information that is reasonably necessary to provide its account, licensing, token, payment, monitoring, and support functionality.

Account and licensing information may include:

  • Email address associated with a TapTap account.
  • Account or activation status.
  • License or account identifier.
  • Device or installation identifier used to associate an extension installation with an account.

Service and token information may include:

  • Token balance.
  • Token consumption records.
  • License validation and synchronization information.
  • Information necessary to prevent duplicate or unauthorized token use.

Payment information may include:

  • Payment transaction reference.
  • Payment amount.
  • Payment status.
  • The TapTap account or license associated with a payment.

TapTap does not collect or store users' M-Pesa PINs, bank passwords, or other banking authentication credentials.

2. Information Stored Locally by the Extension

TapTap uses Chrome's extension storage facilities to store information required for the extension to function. This may include:

  • Account information required by the extension.
  • Activation or license status.
  • Device or installation identifier.
  • Extension preferences.
  • Monitoring settings.
  • Page-refresh or auto-refresh settings.

Locally stored extension information remains within the extension's storage environment on the user's device. Users can remove local extension data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its stored data through Chrome.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

TapTap does not intentionally collect the following information:

  • M-Pesa PINs.
  • Banking passwords or banking authentication credentials.
  • Private messages.
  • Personal files stored on the user's computer.
  • Browsing history unrelated to TapTap's supported website.
  • Unrelated website content.
  • Precise physical location information.

4. Website Monitoring

TapTap operates on supported websites for which the extension is intended to provide its functionality.

The extension monitors relevant page content only to detect information necessary for its intended service, such as transaction or deposit information required to trigger the authorized notification feature.

TapTap does not intentionally monitor unrelated websites or collect unrelated browsing information.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by TapTap may be used to:

  • Authenticate and identify user accounts.
  • Validate licenses and account status.
  • Synchronize token balances.
  • Authorize and record token consumption.
  • Confirm payments and update token balances.
  • Provide TapTap's monitoring and notification functionality.
  • Maintain extension and backend functionality.
  • Prevent unauthorized use, abuse, or duplicate transactions.
  • Maintain service security and reliability.
  • Respond to customer support requests.

We do not use collected information for unrelated advertising or user profiling.

6. Server Communication

TapTap communicates with Batman Tech's backend services over HTTPS. Server communication may be used to:

  • Authenticate accounts.
  • Validate licenses and activation status.
  • Synchronize token balances.
  • Authorize token consumption.
  • Record token usage.
  • Confirm payment transactions.
  • Maintain account and service records.
  • Provide support and service functionality.

The extension does not download or execute remote JavaScript or other executable code as part of its normal operation.

7. Payment Processing

TapTap may use third-party payment or payment-confirmation services, including M-Pesa-related services, to process or confirm purchases.

When a user makes a payment, TapTap may receive transaction information necessary to confirm the payment and associate it with the appropriate TapTap account or license.

TapTap does not request or store a user's M-Pesa PIN.

Third-party payment providers process payment information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

Batman Tech does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate TapTap. These providers may include:

  • Cloud and secure hosting providers.
  • Database and infrastructure providers.
  • Payment-processing or payment-confirmation services.
  • Technical service providers used to maintain TapTap's backend.

Service providers receive only information reasonably necessary for the services they provide.

We do not share personal information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the security of the service, or prevent fraud and abuse.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Account and service information may be stored on Batman Tech's backend infrastructure for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide TapTap and maintain user accounts.
  • Maintain licensing and token records.
  • Maintain payment and transaction records.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized use.
  • Resolve disputes and support requests.
  • Meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

Local extension data remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user, the extension is uninstalled, or the relevant Chrome storage is cleared.

10. Data Security

Batman Tech uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Communication between TapTap and its backend services is protected using HTTPS encryption.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their own devices, accounts, and authentication information.

11. Your Choices and Data Requests

Users may:

  • Stop using TapTap at any time.
  • Uninstall the TapTap extension.
  • Clear locally stored extension data.
  • Contact Batman Tech regarding their account or personal information.
  • Request information about personal data associated with their TapTap account.
  • Ask questions about how their information is processed.

Requests involving account or personal information may require reasonable verification of the requester's identity or account ownership before information is disclosed or an account action is performed.

12. Children's Privacy

TapTap is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

If you believe that information belonging to a child has been provided to Batman Tech improperly, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TapTap's functionality, services, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with its effective date.
