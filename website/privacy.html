Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

TapTap ("the Extension"), developed by Batman Tech, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

The extension may collect the following information:

  • Email address used for account linking.
  • Account activation status.
  • Token balance and synchronization information.
  • Extension preferences stored locally.
  • Payment status for M-Pesa token purchases.

TapTap does not collect passwords, banking credentials, personal messages, browsing history, or unrelated website content.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account.
  • Synchronize token balances across devices.
  • Process token purchases.
  • Maintain extension functionality.
  • Provide customer support.

Website Monitoring

The extension operates only on supported websites selected by the user. It monitors page changes solely to detect new deposit addresses required for its intended functionality.

Local Storage

TapTap uses Chrome Storage to store activation status, and account information necessary for the extension to operate correctly.

Server Communication

TapTap securely communicates with Batman Tech's backend servers over HTTPS to authenticate users, synchronize token balances, validate licenses, and process account requests.

The extension does not download or execute remote JavaScript or other executable code.

Data Sharing

Batman Tech does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is shared only with services necessary to provide the functionality of the extension, such as secure hosting and payment processing.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to secure user information during transmission and storage.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Uninstall the extension at any time.
  • Stop using the service whenever you wish.
  • Contact support regarding your account.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will always be available on this page.
