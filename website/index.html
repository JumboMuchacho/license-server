Frequently Asked Questions

What is TapTap 3.0?+

TapTap is a real-time browser monitoring extension tailored specifically for you...

How does TapTap work?+

After installing the extension and pinning it on your browser, enter your email & top up tokens to activate. TapTap continuously monitors the page and plays an instant audio alert whenever a new deposit address appears.

Can I use TapTap on multiple browsers & devices?+

Yes. Simply sign in using the same registered email and your token balance will be synchronized automatically.

Which browsers are supported?+

TapTap supports Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera and other Chromium-based browsers.

Does TapTap work in the background?+

Yes. Once monitoring has been started, TapTap continues working while you browse, allowing uninterrupted monitoring.

How do I purchase more tokens?+

Click Top Up, choose your preferred token package, enter your M-Pesa number, and approve the STK Push request on your phone.

What are tokens used for?+

Tokens are used to activate monitoring sessions. They are your launch keys to fire up the system. Your balance is synchronized automatically across your linked devices.

What does the Online Users counter mean?+

It shows how many TapTap users are currently connected to the service, providing a live indication that the monitoring network is active.

Is my information secure?+

Yes. TapTap only stores the information necessary for account synchronization and token management. Payments are processed securely through M-Pesa and thats it.

My payment was successful but my tokens haven't appeared.+

Token balances usually update within a few seconds. If not, press Sync or wait briefly. If the problem continues, contact support with your registered email.

I don't hear any alert.+

Ensure monitoring has been started and system color isn't red, your browser isn't muted and your volume is turned up. If the problem persists, contact support.
