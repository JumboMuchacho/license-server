FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TapTap 3.0?

TapTap is a real-time browser monitoring extension designed to notify you instantly whenever a new deposit address appears.

How does TapTap work?

Install the extension, link your email, purchase tokens and start monitoring.

Can I use multiple browsers?

Yes. Your account synchronizes across all supported Chromium browsers.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and other Chromium browsers.

Does TapTap work in the background?

Yes. Monitoring continues while you browse.

How do I buy more tokens?

Choose a package, enter your M-Pesa number and approve the payment request.

What are tokens used for?

Tokens activate monitoring sessions and synchronize across all linked devices.

What does Online Users mean?

It displays how many TapTap clients are currently connected.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Only the information required for synchronization and token management is stored.

Payment succeeded but no tokens?

Wait a few seconds then press Sync. If the issue persists, contact support.

I don't hear the alarm.

Ensure monitoring is active, your browser isn't muted and system volume is enabled.

How do I contact support?

Use the WhatsApp button below or the Help icon inside the extension.
